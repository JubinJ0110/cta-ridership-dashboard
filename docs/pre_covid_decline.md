# The decline before COVID: 2012-2019

Ridership peaked in **2012 at 545.6M rides** and fell every
year but one to **455.7M in 2019 — down 16.5%**
before COVID ever arrived. The pandemic hit a system already
losing riders for seven years.

## Yearly totals (millions)

| Year | Total | Bus | Rail |
|------|------:|----:|-----:|
| 2012 | 545.6 | 314.4 | 231.2 |
| 2013 | 528.8 | 300.1 | 228.7 |
| 2014 | 514.2 | 276.1 | 238.1 |
| 2015 | 516.0 | 274.3 | 241.7 |
| 2016 | 497.7 | 259.1 | 238.6 |
| 2017 | 479.4 | 249.2 | 230.2 |
| 2018 | 468.1 | 242.2 | 225.9 |
| 2019 | 455.7 | 237.3 | 218.5 |

## It was mostly a bus story

- Bus fell 314.4M to 237.3M (-24.5%, 77.1M rides lost).
- Rail fell 231.2M to 218.5M (-5.5%, 12.7M rides lost).
- Bus accounted for 86% of the total decline.

That matters for the recovery story: bus has also recovered
faster than rail since 2020, so the mode that lost the most
before COVID is the one coming back fastest after it.
